# KhanSoft Resizer - Free Online Image & PDF Resizer

A fast, lightweight, and completely private web tool to resize images and compress PDF files directly in your web browser. 

👉 **Live Demo:** [https://ashrafulsiddiquee.github.io/resizer/](https://ashrafulsiddiquee.github.io/resizer/)

---

## 🌟 Why KhanSoft Resizer?

Many online tools upload your private files to their remote servers. KhanSoft Resizer works 100% locally on your computer, tablet, or phone. 

* **Complete Privacy:** Your photos and PDF files are never uploaded to the internet or stored on a server.
* **Instant Processing:** All scaling and compression happen immediately on your device.
* **Zero Cost:** 100% free with no registration, no watermarks, and no file limits.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Three Easy Size Options:**
  * **Low Resolution:** Resizes up to 800px. Perfect for email attachments, message apps, and very small file sizes.
  * **Medium Resolution:** Resizes up to 1400px. Best balance between small file size and clear picture quality.
  * **High Resolution:** Resizes up to 2400px. Keeps maximum detail for printing and high-definition screens.
* **Supports Common Formats:**
  * Images: JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP
  * Documents: Multi-page PDF files
* **Theme Support:** Clean Dark Mode and Light Mode with automatic theme memory.
* **Responsive Design:** Works smoothly on desktop computers, laptops, iPads, tablets, and smartphones.
* **Instant Statistics:** View your original file size, new file size, and space saved in real time.

## 🛠️ How to Use

1. Open [https://ashrafulsiddiquee.github.io/resizer/](https://ashrafulsiddiquee.github.io/resizer/).
2. Drag and drop your image or PDF file into the upload box (or click to browse).
3. Select your desired preset: **Low**, **Medium**, or **High**.
4. Click **Resize & Download** to save your optimized file immediately.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Are my files safe?
Yes. The app works entirely inside your web browser. No photos, documents, or personal data ever leave your device.

### Can I resize multi-page PDF documents?
Yes. The app processes each page of your PDF, adjusts the size, and puts everything back together into a single downloadable PDF.

### What is the best option for job or visa applications?
Most application websites ask for small file sizes (often below 500 KB). Choose the **Low** or **Medium** preset for these forms.
